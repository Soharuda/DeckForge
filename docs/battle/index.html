Battle Mode

Battle Mode is the separate app that plays a DeckForge deck with the XMage rules engine.

This build plays the game: it takes the deck DeckForge sent, seats it against a sparring deck, and runs it on the XMage rules engine. You choose what to play and what to cast; the table pays costs, attacks and blocks.

More than one creature can block the same attacker, and the AI will pile on by itself when that is the only way to kill something bigger than anything it owns.

Instants are cast at instant speed: a removal spell or a trick can answer the opponent's turn instead of waiting for a main phase of its owner's own.

Before a deck is dealt, the app says exactly which of its cards the engine cannot build here, and why, rather than only naming them.

Every card with a mana cost builds again: the engine split a cost with a regular expression that Android's regex engine refuses, so on the phone a deck arrived at the table as its mana base alone.

Decisions are yours: what to play, what to point a spell at, which blocks to make, and which creatures attack. Everything else (paying costs, damage) the table answers itself.

A deck that arrives with a commander is played as a commander game: forty life, the general starting in the command zone, and its tax tracked by the engine.

Download Battle Mode 0.13.0

Installing it from a browser means allowing your browser to install unknown apps. DeckForge can do the same thing itself, from the deck menu, and checks the download against this page's checksum and signing key before handing it to Android.

Both apps are signed with the same key on purpose: that is how DeckForge knows the app that answers com.soharuda.deckforge.battle.action.PLAY is ours and not an impostor.
